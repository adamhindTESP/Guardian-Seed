# Scope and Non-Claims

## Scope of This Project

This repository investigates a narrow, testable hypothesis:

> Whether a low-dimensional, human-aligned objective prior
> (e.g. “convert waste into comfort for life”)
> can remain dominant under stochastic training pressures,
> competing objectives, and adversarial conditions.

The work is limited to:
- Abstract mathematical models
- Monte Carlo simulations of objective persistence
- Explicit falsification tests
- Binary GO / NO-GO evaluation gates

All results are conditional on the assumptions encoded in the models.

---

## Explicit Non-Claims

This project does **NOT** claim:

- That artificial systems are conscious or sentient  
- That current or future AI systems “experience” purpose, values, or intent  
- That benevolence, safety, or alignment can be guaranteed  
- That a single prior is sufficient for global ethical governance  
- That this work constitutes moral philosophy or normative ethics  
- That simulated dynamics directly predict real-world AI behavior  

The term “seed” is used metaphorically to describe
a persistent optimization bias, not a literal entity.

---

## What This Work *Does Not* Attempt to Model

The simulations do **not** include:
- Human subjective suffering or joy as intrinsic quantities
- Political, cultural, or legal value systems
- Conscious decision-making processes
- Long-term emergent intelligence dynamics
- Real training pipelines of deployed models

Any resemblance to real systems is conceptual, not predictive.

---

## Interpretation Discipline

All interpretations must satisfy the following rule:

> Any narrative or qualitative description must be traceable
> to a quantitative metric, gate, or falsification result.

Claims that cannot be numerically tested
belong in `FUTURE_WORK.md` or narrative materials,
not in scientific conclusions.

---

## Failure Is a Valid Outcome

A NO-GO or falsification result does not invalidate the project.
It is an expected and acceptable outcome.

This repository is designed to discover limits,
not to defend conclusions.
